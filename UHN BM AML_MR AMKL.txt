A del(5q) is suspected based on the discordant signals between 5p and 5q: 4 signals for 5p and 2 for 5q.
Unequivocal evidence for a deletion of TP53 is lacking (twoTP53 signals are observed), although it is difficult to determine in a triploid/tetraploid setting. Please refer to separate report (B22-17870). Note that one TP53 mutation at VAF 31% was detected by NGS.
Based on the additional results, the diagnosis is most consistent with AML, myelodysplasia-related (previously AML with MRC).
