Diagnosis
Peripheral blood demonstrates mild rouleaux.
Bone marrow aspirate is dilute and flow cytometry demonstrates a tiny monotypic plasma cell population within a larger polytypic plasma cell population. No monotypic B cell population detected.
The biopsy is 0.6cm and demonstrates no normal hematopoiesis. Half of the biopsy demonstrates fibrous scar tissue with possible previous necrosis. The other half demonstrates severe collagen fibrosis with scattered small lymphocytes, macrophages, histiocytes and endothelial cells. There are scattered mononuclear cells of which many are degenerated and not clearly morphologically interpretable. IHC for plasma cells markers (CD138/kappa/lambda and CD117) and B cell marker (CD20) are compromised by severe back ground staining which renders them not interpretable. No increase in CD34 blasts, tryptase mast cells, CD30 cells, keratin solid tumor cells and congo red is negative.
Given isolated mild-moderate anemia on the peripheral blood sample, and the fact that no hematopoietic marrow is seen on the sample supplied, this biopsy likely represents a local phenomenon (ie, unlikely that the entire marrow would have no hematopoietic tissue and demonstrate severe collagen fibrosis).
No further diagnosis can be made on this sample. It is recommended to repeat bone marrow with flow cytometry and biopsy in a different location from this biopsy sample to determine the underlying process.
