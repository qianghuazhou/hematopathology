NYGH Hematopathology Molecular Testing Workflow (2026)

MYELOBLASTS ≥20% (Suspected AML)

Initial investigations

  • CBC and peripheral blood smear
  • Bone marrow aspirate and biopsy
  • Standard AML flow cytometry
  • Conventional cytogenetics (karyotype)
  • NYGH 42-Gene Myeloid NGS Panel
  • Targeted molecular/FISH studies as clinically indicated:
    • PML::RARA
    • RUNX1::RUNX1T1
    • CBFB::MYH11
    • KMT2A rearrangement
    • MECOM rearrangement
    • NUP98 rearrangement
    • BCR::ABL1

AML, Myelodysplasia-Related

Defining cytogenetic abnormalities

  • Complex karyotype
  • del(5q)
  • −7/del(7q)
  • del(11q)
  • del(12p)
  • −13/del(13q)
  • 17p abnormalities / i(17q)

Defining gene mutations

ASXL1, BCOR, EZH2, RUNX1, SF3B1, SRSF2, STAG2, U2AF1, ZRSR2

Initial investigations
  • Flow cytometry

  • NYGH 42-Gene Myeloid NGS Panel
→
Evaluate for
  • AML with defining genetic abnormalities
  • MDS with increased blasts (WHO-HAEM5)
  • AML/MDS overlap according to ICC, where applicable

CYTOPENIA (<5% Blasts)

Initial investigations

  • Bone marrow morphology
  • Conventional cytogenetics
  • NYGH 42-Gene Myeloid NGS Panel

Reflex MDS FISH when indicated

  • Failed or inadequate karyotype
  • Insufficient metaphases
  • Targeted confirmation of a suspected abnormality

Interpretation

  • If morphology is diagnostic, classify according to WHO-HAEM5.
  • If morphology is not diagnostic but pathogenic myeloid-associated mutation(s) are detected, consider CCUS after clinicopathologic correlation.
