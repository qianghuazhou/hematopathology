TRISS

TRISS Trials Group, NEJM, 2014

Population: Adult ICU patients with septic shock and hemoglobin ≤9 g/dL.

Comparison: Restrictive threshold 70 g/L vs liberal threshold 90 g/L.

  • No difference in 90-day mortality between strategies.
  • Restrictive strategy used substantially fewer RBC transfusions.
  • No difference in life support use, ischemic events, or complications.
Take-home: A 70 g/L transfusion threshold is safe in septic shock and reduces blood use without increasing mortality.
