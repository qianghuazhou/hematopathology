Comments:
Thank you for sharing this very interesting case of a patient with mild thrombocytosis, few large and rare giant platelets. No information found regarding spleen and liver size.
NGS data show a pathogenic/likely pathogenic SH2B3 variant (Glu395Lys), with an AF 0f 53%, in addition to a second SH2B3 variant of uncertain significance (Glu400Lys), AF 45%. SH2B3 mutations have been described in triple negative MPNs, and in association with the usual driving mutations in MPN. SH2B3 products are a negative regulator of cytokine-receptor mediated JAK2/STAT5 signalling and its mutations with loss of function may produce cytosis, MPN or familial MPN.
In this patient’s case, the association of SH2B3 variant with thrombocytosis, bone marrow hypercellularity, moderate megakaryocytic hyperplasia and mild morphological atypia, even in the absence of overt features of MPN, is likely relevant and may indicate early morphologic stages of ET. A family history of thrombocytosis should be sought. Of note, the NGS methodology used at UHN does not allow the distinction between germline and somatic mutations of the genes in the HEME panel.
Paz DC et al, Blood (2023) 141 (16): 1909-1921
