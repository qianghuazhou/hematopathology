Pattern-based morphology • differential diagnosis • immunohistochemistry • diagnostic pitfalls • report language

Scope: Reactive, infectious, inflammatory, histiocytic, and incidental lymph node findings. Malignant mimics are included as safety checkpoints. Interpret with clinical history, imaging, tissue adequacy, and ancillary studies. Reference content is educational and requires case-specific verification.

Sources and editorial notes

Primary organization adapted from the user-provided teaching handout Lymph Nodes, Non-Lymphoma (Kurt Schaberg, 2022; based on a Brent Tan lecture) and Session 16 Flow, Genetic, LN notes. Descriptions are paraphrased and condensed, not reproduced verbatim. No histology images from the teaching PDF have been republished. Verify evolving disease classifications and test performance against current primary literature and local protocols before clinical use.

Important: A negative flow cytometry result does not independently exclude lymphoma; limited biopsies can miss focal disease. Special stains may be negative despite infection. Clinical correlation and microbiology testing are appropriate when indicated.
