Benign Non-Lymphoid Inclusions in Lymph Nodes

Quick diagnostic reference: morphology, location, immunophenotype, and malignant mimics. Immunostains are supportive, not definitive in isolation.

Types and differential diagnosis

Reporting guidance

Benign inclusion: “Benign [type] inclusion within lymph node. No evidence of metastatic malignancy.”

Indeterminate thyroid follicles: “Thyroid follicular tissue identified in lymph node. Metastatic thyroid carcinoma cannot be excluded on morphology and immunophenotype alone; recommend thyroid evaluation and correlation with imaging.”

For unexpected epithelial, melanocytic, or vascular elements, consider tissue levels, prior procedures, known malignancy, nodal distribution, and cytologic atypia before labeling as benign.
