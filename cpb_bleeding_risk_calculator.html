Method notes

  • The paper defined bleeding risk by total blood component use within 48 hours from start of surgery.
  • Original study categories: low ≤13.5 units, intermediate 13.5–22.5 units, high ≥22.5 units.
  • Final 5-input model: platelet count, TEG-R, PT, D-dimer, and TEG-CI.
  • This implementation assigns points to abnormality severity and maps the total score to low, intermediate, or high predicted bleeding/transfusion risk.
Reference: Huang RSP et al. Post-Operative Bleeding Risk Stratification in Cardiac Pulmonary Bypass Patients Using Artificial Neural Network. Ann Clin Lab Sci. 2015;45(2):181-186.
