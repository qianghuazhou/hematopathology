Interpretation and limitations

Document specimen type, viability, cellularity, event counts, population size, immunophenotype and clinically relevant limitations. Interpret small or discordant populations with morphology, histology and molecular findings.

A negative or nondiagnostic flow result does not exclude lymphoma when tissue sampling is limited or specimen viability is poor.

Educational and reporting support only. Apply validated local panels and laboratory procedures.
