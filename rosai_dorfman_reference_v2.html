Diagnostic essence

Pattern

Nodal RDD: preserved but distorted architecture with sinus expansion by pale histiocytes, lymphocytes, and plasma cells.

Cell

Large histiocytes with abundant pale/eosinophilic cytoplasm, vesicular nuclei, distinct nucleoli, and emperipolesis.

Phenotype

S100+, OCT2+, CD68/CD163+, often cyclin D1/pERK+; CD1a-, langerin-.

Practical point: Focal S100-positive sinus histiocytes without diagnostic emperipolesis are not sufficient for RDD. Correlate morphology, IHC, site, and clinical behavior.

Morphology

Nodal disease

  • Architecture intact but distorted.
  • Marked sinus dilatation.
  • Large RDD histiocytes in sinuses.
  • Emperipolesis of viable lymphocytes/plasma cells/neutrophils/RBCs.
  • Often many plasma cells and reactive follicles.
  • Sclerosis may be present.

Extranodal disease

  • May involve skin/subcutis, bone, sinonasal tract, orbit, soft tissue, dura/CNS, kidney, breast, and others.
  • No sinusoidal pattern outside lymph node.
  • RDD histiocytes can be sparse.
  • Emperipolesis may be focal or absent.
  • Fibrosis/plasmacytosis may dominate.

Workup and reporting pearls

When to consider molecular testing

Molecular testing for MAPK pathway alterations may be considered in multifocal, recurrent, refractory, extranodal, or clinically aggressive disease, particularly if targeted therapy is contemplated.

Report pitfalls

  • Do not diagnose RDD from S100 positivity alone.
  • Emperipolesis is supportive but may be subtle or absent in extranodal disease.
  • IgG4-rich plasma cells can occur in RDD and are not specific for IgG4-RD.
  • Exclude lymphoma, LCH, ECD, infection, and metastatic malignancy as clinically indicated.
