Project 01 · Ongoing

Optical Genome Mapping (OGM)

Clinical and Diagnostic Utility of Optical Genome Mapping in Hematologic Malignancies

NYGH-led collaborative retrospective real-world study with Sunnybrook Health Sciences Centre, evaluating OGM performed in routine hematologic malignancy assessment from May 2024 onward.

  • Diagnostic yield and incremental findings beyond conventional karyotyping and FISH
  • Structural variants, copy-number changes, cryptic and complex abnormalities
  • Impact on WHO-HAEM5/ICC classification and prognostic risk stratification
  • Laboratory performance, quality metrics and turnaround time

Current focus: Consolidation of NYGH and Sunnybrook data, integrated case review and protocol/ethics development.

Project 02 · Research study

COMPASS Study

Clinical Optical Mapping for Precision Assessment of Structural Variants

Investigator-initiated retrospective study of the clinical and diagnostic utility of optical genome mapping in hematologic malignancies, using existing clinical and laboratory records.

  • Distribution of OGM result categories and clinically relevant findings not resolved by conventional cytogenetic testing
  • Concordance with karyotype/FISH and characterization of structural and copy-number abnormalities
  • Potential effects on disease classification, risk assignment and clinical management
  • Exploratory analysis of technical feasibility and clinical workflow

Study approach: Retrospective coded data analysis; no additional patient procedures or testing.
