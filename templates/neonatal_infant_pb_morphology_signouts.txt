6. IMPORTANT CAUTIONS

- Do not attribute significant anemia, marked polychromasia, or neutropenia solely to prematurity.
- Interpret ANC against gestational- and postnatal-age-specific intervals, especially in the first 72 hours.
- ICSH schistocyte reference limits: <=1% for term and <=5% for preterm neonates; interpret in context.
- NRBC significance depends strongly on gestational and postnatal age.
- Concerning blasts require assessment; a 20% threshold alone does not exclude congenital leukemia or transient abnormal myelopoiesis.
