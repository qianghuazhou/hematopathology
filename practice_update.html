What this page will track

This launch edition establishes the structure and priority watch areas. It is not yet a verified weekly literature digest. New items should be added only after checking publication dates, study design and original sources. Each future update will include a concise finding, strength of evidence, impact on practice and a direct source link.

01 · Transfusion medicine

Transfusion medicine

Clinical transfusion, blood components and transfusion service operations

Priority watch areas

  • RBC, platelet, plasma and cryoprecipitate thresholds
  • Massive hemorrhage and pediatric MHP
  • Transfusion reactions: TACO, TRALI, hemolysis and TA-GVHD
  • Sickle cell disease, alloimmunization and antigen matching
  • IVIG, albumin, plasma exchange and PBM
  • Canadian Blood Services and AABB guidance
Future weekly entries

New recommendations and trials will be summarized with direct implications for blood bank policy and transfusion consultation.

Source hubs: Canadian Blood Services · AABB · PubMed
02 · Coagulation medicine

Coagulation medicine

Hemostasis, thrombosis and coagulation laboratory interpretation

Priority watch areas

  • DOACs, reversal agents and perioperative management
  • VTE, cancer-associated thrombosis and thromboprophylaxis
  • DIC, TMA, HIT and antiphospholipid syndrome
  • Factor deficiencies, inhibitors and von Willebrand disease
  • PT/aPTT, mixing studies, lupus anticoagulant testing
  • Fibrinogen, viscoelastic assays and FXI inhibitors
Research topic to watch

Factor XI/XIa inhibition: follow new trial outcomes and the balance between thrombosis prevention and bleeding risk. Do not infer clinical adoption from early mechanistic results.

03 · Hematopathology diagnosis

Hematopathology diagnosis

Diagnostic classification, molecular pathology and practical reporting

Priority watch areas

  • WHO-HAEM5 and ICC classification refinements
  • AML, MDS, MPN and clonal cytopenias
  • B-cell and T/NK-cell lymphoma diagnostics
  • Flow cytometry, IHC and molecular/FISH workup
  • Optical genome mapping and genomic technologies
  • AI-assisted marrow and lymph node diagnosis
Conference / research watch

The Society for Hematopathology's 2026 program includes AI-based bone marrow diagnostics, myeloid classification and myeloid neoplasms with fibrosis. Conference topics are not themselves new diagnostic guidelines.

How weekly updates will be written

Each selected paper or guideline

  • What changed: 2–3 sentence summary
  • Evidence: guideline, randomized trial, cohort, review or preprint
  • Practice relevance: specific laboratory or clinical action
  • Limitations: uncertainty, conflicting data or implementation barriers
  • Source: original article, DOI/PMID and publication date

Editorial priorities

  • Prefer primary studies and professional society guidance
  • Separate new evidence from established background
  • Prioritize Canada/Ontario implementation when relevant
  • Flag potential SOP, report-template and diagnostic algorithm changes
  • Record when there is no meaningful update
