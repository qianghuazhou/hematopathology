How to use

1. Pick a disease or situation from the left menu.

2. Click Load example from library to populate a realistic example immunophenotype and flow analysis structure.

3. Replace percentages, absolute counts, light chain, and marker details with case-specific findings.

4. Generate and copy the final report.

This tool intentionally separates sign-out wording from reference immunophenotype. The reference section is for memory support and should be edited before final sign-out.

General limitations

Flow cytometry is an adjunct to morphology and may not reliably classify all lymphomas or estimate blast burden in all specimens. Correlate with tissue architecture, cytology, histology, IHC, cytogenetic and molecular studies.

Use caution with low event count, low viability, hemodilute marrow aspirate, necrotic tissue, CSF/body fluid samples, Hodgkin lymphoma, large B-cell lymphoma, and non-hematopoietic tumors.
