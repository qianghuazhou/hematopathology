Specimen-first workflow

1. Select specimen type: PB, BM, LN/Tissue, Body Fluid, CSF, BAL/Respiratory, or Nondiagnostic.

2. Select disease/situation category within that specimen.

3. Load example values, revise percentages and markers, then generate the report.

This layout mirrors sign-out workflow: specimen first, then disease category, with specimen-specific limitations and comments.

General limitations

Flow cytometry is an adjunct to morphology. It may not reliably classify all lymphomas or estimate blast burden in all specimens. Correlate with cytology, histology, IHC, cytogenetics, molecular studies and clinical findings.
