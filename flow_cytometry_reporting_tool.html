Use notes

This tool combines a broad disease-specific flow template library with more standardized modern canned comments. CPT coding and billing language have been removed.

Suggested workflow: report a short diagnosis line, then a compact interpretive comment. Avoid overcalling on low event counts, poor viability, hemodilute marrow aspirates, necrotic tissue, or body fluid samples with very few leukocytes.

General limitation language

Flow cytometry is an adjunct to morphology and may not reliably classify all lymphomas or estimate blast burden in all specimens. Correlation with histology, cytology, clinical findings, cytogenetic and molecular studies is recommended.
