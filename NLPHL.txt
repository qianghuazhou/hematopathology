Morphological description
This lymph node shows partial effacement of node architecture with predominantly vaguely nodular proliferation composed of scattered large atypical lymphocyte-predominant (LP) cells with multilobated ("popcorn-like") nuclei, irregular nuclear contours, and small but distinct nucleoli. The nodules show further many small lymphocytes. No significant fibrosis is seen. The capsule is represented.
