p53 IHC Image Pattern Estimator

Browser-only visual assistant for p53 nuclear/cytoplasmic staining pattern estimation. Not for standalone diagnostic use.

1. Case / image

Best input: representative tumor-rich field or hotspot. Avoid necrosis, folds, crushed tissue, edge artifact, and non-tumor-rich areas.

2. Thresholds

This is a heuristic estimator. p53 interpretation still requires visual review of tumor nuclei, internal controls, and tissue context.

Report text

Important limitation: This tool estimates staining distribution from RGB image features. It does not reliably separate tumor from benign epithelium, lymphocytes, stroma, necrosis, melanin, hemosiderin, crush artifact, or poor fixation. Final classification should be based on pathologist review and internal controls.
