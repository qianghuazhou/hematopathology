Fungal Histopathology & Mimics

Practical tissue morphology, diagnostic clues and special stains. Each organism includes a simplified schematic (not a photomicrograph or scale drawing).

Diagnostic caution: Morphology often supports presumptive identification, not species confirmation. Correlate with culture, PCR/sequencing, antigen testing, epidemiology and immune status. Damaged molds may show atypical branching or septation.
