Aggressive Large B-Cell Lymphoma Diagnostic Approach

A practical sign-out reference for large B-cell / high-grade B-cell lymphomas: first recognize an aggressive B-cell neoplasm, then separate DLBCL, Burkitt lymphoma, high-grade B-cell lymphoma, blastoid mantle cell lymphoma, and selected special entities using morphology, IHC, and FISH.

Overview

This page is intentionally focused on the practical approach to an aggressive large B-cell lymphoma biopsy. It is not a full lymphoma encyclopedia. The main workflow is: morphology → core B-cell phenotype → Burkitt-like vs DLBCL-like → exclude blastoid mantle cell lymphoma → order FISH → finalize classification.

High-Yield Points

  • Do not finalize DLBCL, NOS before MYC/BCL2/BCL6 FISH if high-grade features are present.
  • Burkitt-like morphology requires MYC, BCL2/BCL6, and 11q correlation.
  • CD5/Cyclin D1/SOX11 positivity should trigger exclusion of blastoid mantle cell lymphoma.
  • Double expresser is IHC; double-hit is genetic.

Overview

DLBCL-like

Diffuse large B-cell lymphoma, NOS

Diffuse sheets of large atypical B cells; classify by Hans algorithm and exclude HGBL with MYC and BCL2 and/or BCL6 rearrangements.

Burkitt-like

Burkitt lymphoma / HGBL

Intermediate-sized monomorphic cells, starry-sky pattern, very high Ki-67; requires FISH correlation including MYC and 11q.

CD5 / Cyclin D1

Blastoid / pleomorphic mantle cell lymphoma

Can mimic aggressive LBCL. Confirm with Cyclin D1, SOX11, and CCND1/IGH FISH.

Practical framing: in a core biopsy showing a high-grade B-cell infiltrate, a preliminary diagnosis is often safer until FISH is available.

Diagnostic Pitfalls

Double expresser vs double hit

Double expresser is MYC/BCL2 protein overexpression by IHC. Double hit requires gene rearrangements by FISH.

Burkitt-like is not Burkitt

Burkitt-like morphology should trigger FISH rather than immediate final classification.

CD20-negative recurrence

After anti-CD20 therapy, use PAX5, CD79a, CD19, OCT2/BOB1, and clinical history.

Limited core or FNA

Use “aggressive B-cell lymphoma” wording if architecture and full classification cannot be assessed.
