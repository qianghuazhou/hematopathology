EXTRANODAL LYMPHOMA / LYMPHOID CONSULT MORPHOLOGY CHEAT SHEET
STOMACH
Normal

Gastric mucosa with preserved foveolar and glandular architecture. The lamina propria contains scattered chronic inflammatory cells without significant expansion.

Chronic gastritis

The lamina propria is expanded by lymphocytes and plasma cells. Reactive lymphoid follicles with germinal centres may be present.

Reactive follicle

Well-formed lymphoid follicles with polarized germinal centres, tingible body macrophages, and preserved mantle zones.

MALT lymphoma

Dense infiltrates of small atypical lymphocytes expand the lamina propria with infiltration of glandular epithelium forming lymphoepithelial lesions. Residual glands are entrapped within the infiltrate.

DLBCL

Diffuse infiltrates of large atypical lymphoid cells efface the mucosa and infiltrate the submucosa. Tumour cells exhibit vesicular chromatin, prominent nucleoli and frequent mitotic figures.

SMALL BOWEL
Normal

Preserved villous architecture with intact villous-to-crypt ratio.

Reactive

Lamina propria contains mixed inflammatory cells with preserved architecture.

Celiac-type injury

Villous blunting with crypt hyperplasia and increased intraepithelial lymphocytes.

Lymphoma

Diffuse expansion of the lamina propria and/or submucosa by atypical lymphoid cells with distortion of normal mucosal architecture.

EATL

Transmural infiltrates of pleomorphic atypical lymphoid cells associated with ulceration, necrosis and inflammatory debris.

COLON
Reactive lymphoid aggregate

Well-circumscribed lymphoid aggregate with preserved germinal centre formation and no significant cytologic atypia.

Reactive lymphoid hyperplasia

Prominent lymphoid follicles with preserved architecture and reactive germinal centres.

Lymphoma

Diffuse or nodular infiltrates of atypical lymphoid cells expand the lamina propria and may distort crypt architecture.

SALIVARY GLAND
Normal

Salivary gland tissue composed of acini and ductal structures arranged in lobules.

Chronic sialadenitis

Lymphoplasmacytic infiltrates surround ducts and acini with variable acinar loss.

Sjogren pattern

Dense periductal lymphoid infiltrates with reactive follicle formation and acinar atrophy.

MALT lymphoma

Dense infiltrates of small atypical lymphocytes replace salivary gland parenchyma with residual entrapped ducts and acini.

THYROID
Hashimoto thyroiditis

Diffuse lymphoplasmacytic infiltrates with numerous reactive germinal centres and oncocytic follicular epithelial change.

Thyroid lymphoma

Extensive replacement of thyroid parenchyma by atypical lymphoid cells with destruction of native follicles.

LUNG
Normal

Preserved alveolated lung parenchyma with delicate alveolar septa.

Reactive

Patchy chronic inflammatory infiltrates involving alveolar septa and bronchovascular structures.

MALT lymphoma

Dense lymphoid infiltrates expand bronchovascular bundles and interstitium with preservation of overall lung architecture.

DLBCL

Sheets of large atypical lymphoid cells replace alveolated lung parenchyma.

Lymphoid interstitial pneumonia

Diffuse widening of alveolar septa by mature lymphocytes and plasma cells.

LIVER
Normal

Preserved hepatic lobular architecture with unremarkable portal tracts.

Chronic hepatitis

Portal tracts expanded by lymphocytic infiltrates with variable interface activity.

Reactive lymphoid aggregate

Small portal tract lymphoid aggregate without significant cytologic atypia.

Lymphoma

Portal tracts and adjacent hepatic parenchyma are infiltrated by atypical lymphoid cells.

Sinusoidal pattern

Atypical lymphoid cells preferentially involve hepatic sinusoids.

KIDNEY
Normal

Renal cortex with preserved glomeruli and tubules.

Lymphoma

Diffuse interstitial infiltrates of atypical lymphoid cells separate renal tubules while largely preserving glomeruli.

SKIN
Superficial perivascular infiltrate

Small lymphocytes are distributed around superficial dermal vessels.

Superficial and deep perivascular infiltrate

Lymphoid infiltrates involve both superficial and deep vascular plexuses.

Periadnexal infiltrate

Lymphocytes surround adnexal structures including hair follicles and sweat glands.

Nodular dermal infiltrate

Dense lymphoid aggregates expand the dermis.

Diffuse dermal infiltrate

The dermis is extensively replaced by lymphoid cells.

Grenz zone

A narrow uninvolved zone separates the infiltrate from the overlying epidermis.

Epidermotropism

Lymphocytes infiltrate the epidermis singly and in small collections.

Pautrier microabscess

Small intraepidermal collections of atypical lymphocytes.

BREAST
Normal

Breast tissue composed of terminal duct lobular units embedded within fibroadipose stroma.

Reactive

Mixed inflammatory infiltrates without significant architectural distortion.

Lymphoma

Atypical lymphoid cells infiltrate breast parenchyma and permeate fibrofatty tissue. Native ducts and lobules are entrapped but preserved.

SOFT TISSUE
Reactive

Fibroadipose tissue contains scattered lymphoid aggregates.

Lymphoma

Diffuse infiltrates of atypical lymphoid cells permeate fibroadipose tissue.

Adipocyte permeation

Atypical lymphoid cells infiltrate between individual adipocytes.

Indian-file pattern

Single atypical lymphoid cells infiltrate between adipocytes in linear arrays.

SPLEEN
Normal

Preserved white pulp and red pulp compartments.

Splenic marginal zone lymphoma

White pulp nodules are expanded by small atypical lymphocytes with extension into adjacent red pulp.

Hairy cell leukemia

Diffuse red pulp expansion by small to intermediate-sized lymphoid cells with abundant pale cytoplasm.

BONE
Reactive

Preserved marrow spaces with trilineage hematopoiesis.

Lymphoma

Marrow spaces infiltrated by atypical lymphoid cells in an interstitial, nodular, diffuse or paratrabecular distribution.

IMPORTANT SURGICAL PATHOLOGY TERMS
Entrapped glands

Native glandular structures are surrounded by infiltrating lymphoid cells but retain overall architecture.

Lymphoepithelial lesion

Lymphoid cells infiltrate and partially destroy epithelial structures.

Follicular colonization

Neoplastic lymphocytes infiltrate and expand pre-existing reactive follicles.

Architectural effacement

Normal tissue architecture is partially or completely replaced by infiltrating cells.

Angiocentric

The infiltrate surrounds blood vessels.

Angiodestructive

The infiltrate invades and destroys vascular walls.

Perineural invasion

Tumour cells track along or surround nerve fibres.

Peribronchiolar

Surrounding bronchioles.

Periportal

Surrounding portal tracts.

Interstitial pattern

Cells infiltrate between native tissue elements.

Nodular pattern

Discrete lymphoid nodules are present.

Diffuse pattern

Confluent infiltrate without preservation of normal architecture.

Desmoplasia

Dense collagenized stromal response associated with infiltrating cells.

Fibrosis

Increased deposition of collagen within the involved tissue.

Sclerosis

Dense collagen deposition resulting in architectural distortion.

VERY COMMON "SAFE" SURGPATH PHRASES
"Residual native structures are entrapped within the infiltrate."
"The infiltrate expands but does not completely efface the native architecture."
"The infiltrate demonstrates a nodular to diffuse growth pattern."
"The infiltrate permeates the surrounding stroma."
"The infiltrate extends between pre-existing tissue structures."
"No definitive lymphoepithelial lesions are identified."
"Reactive germinal centres with preserved polarization and tingible body macrophages are present."
"The overall architecture is preserved."
"The overall architecture is partially effaced."
"The overall architecture is extensively effaced by the infiltrate."

These phrases cover probably 80–90% of extranodal lymphoma consult morphology descriptions encountered in routine hematopathology practice.
