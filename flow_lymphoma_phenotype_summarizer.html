Flow Cytometry — Lymphoma Phenotype Summarizer

Local, review-required drafting aid. Extracts an abnormal B-cell phenotype from an imported report without generating a lymphoma diagnosis. Do not upload identifiable patient reports to a public repository.

TXT, PDF (text-based), DOCX, PNG or JPG. Files are processed in your browser. Image OCR and PDF/DOCX extraction load open-source libraries from a CDN; do not use with identifiable patient data unless your institution approves the software and its dependencies.

Quality controls: explicitly detected positive and negative markers only; light-chain restriction and population percentages are included only when documented. Ambiguous/variable markers are flagged. This tool does not infer CD5/CD10 status from a diagnosis, determine Hans cell of origin, or replace flow report review.
